About the shop

Whitfill Machine & Welding is a machine and fabrication shop on Highway 401 in Hudson, Kentucky. Machining, welding, engineering, and assembly all happen in the same building, so a job does not have to move between shops to get finished, and there is one person to call about it.

What we can do for you

Seven capabilities under one roof, so a job can be designed, cut, machined, welded, and assembled in one place.

  • Welding, fabrication & assemblyRepair work, weldments, and finished assemblies

  • CNC turningProduction and short-run lathe work
