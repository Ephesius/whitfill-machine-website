Services

Below are the services that we offer. Feel free to call with any questions.

Welding, fabrication & assembly

Repair welding, built-up weldments, and finished assemblies. A cracked bracket, a worn edge that needs rebuilding, a frame missing a section, or a part built from scratch off your design or ours. Cutting, machining, and welding all happen here in our shop.

CNC milling

Vertical and horizontal machining centers, programmed using MasterCam. Can be used for production runs or one-offs.

CNC turning

Turned parts made to print. Shafts, sleeves, bushings, pins, adapters, and any othere turning needs. Great for production runs and one-offs alike.

Manual milling

Vertical mills for work that does not justify writing a program. Modifying a part that already exists, cutting a keyway, squaring up a casting, building a fixture, or getting a single piece finished quickly.

Manual lathe

Turning and repair work on manual lathes.

Engineering & CAD

Design work and drawings. If you have a part but no print, we measure it, model it, and produce a drawing. We also do original design. If you have an idea, we can design it, model it, and create drawings for it whether it's a sinlge part or a full machine assembly.

Plasma cutting

Plate and sheet cut to shape. Sometimes that is the finished part, more often it is a blank that goes on to be machined or welded here. Send a DXF if you have one, or a drawing and we will make the file.
